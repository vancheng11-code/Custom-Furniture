
Furniture OEM projects usually begin with an idea, drawing, reference product, or existing design. Turning that idea into a production-ready item requires several steps.

Start by defining the dimensions and materials. Wood species, boards, metal components, upholstery, hardware, and surface treatments should all be specified.

Next, provide drawings or reference images whenever possible. Detailed visual information reduces misunderstandings between the buyer and manufacturer.

A prototype or sample should then be produced. This is the stage where dimensions, construction, appearance, comfort, and finish can be evaluated.

Once the sample is approved, the specifications should be documented for mass production.

Packaging also deserves attention, particularly for furniture exported over long distances.
